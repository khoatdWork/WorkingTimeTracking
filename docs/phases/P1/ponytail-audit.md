What this repo does: Tracks a personal monthly work-hour target with office/WFH days. Assumed a few years of personal records, not an official attendance authority.

Checked start/stop/manual edit → validator → local/cloud repository → totals → export, plus pause/midnight/month boundaries. Integer seconds, one lunch per calendar date and future targets separated from elapsed deficit are consistent across views. Overlap and future work rejected. State never seeds fake records.

Known decision: device clock is the source, settings recalculate history, one owner document has850KB app cap; upgrade to documents by month before reaching the limit. Request status is self-reported. No unresolved must-fix finding after fixes in review.

Verdict: healthy at stated personal scale.
Not checked: real devices, independent audit.
